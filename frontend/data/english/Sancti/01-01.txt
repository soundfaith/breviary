[Lectio4]
A sermon of Saint Leo the Pope  
!Sermon 7 on the Nativity of the Lord  
The true worshipper and devout observer of today’s feast, beloved, is he who thinks nothing false about the Lord’s Incarnation, nor anything unworthy about His Divinity. For it is an equal danger if either the truth of our nature is denied to Him, or the equality of the Father’s glory. Therefore, when we approach to understand the mystery of Christ’s birth, by which He was born of a Virgin Mother, let the darkness of earthly reasoning be driven far away, and let the smoke of worldly wisdom depart from the eye of enlightened faith.

[Lectio5]
For the authority we believe is divine; the doctrine we follow is divine. Whether we listen inwardly to the testimony of the Law, or to the oracles of the prophets, or to the trumpet of the Gospel, it is true what John, full of the Holy Spirit, proclaimed: In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God. This was in the beginning with God. All things were made through Him, and without Him was made nothing. And likewise it is true what the same preacher added: The Word was made flesh and dwelt among us; and we saw His glory, the glory as of the Only‑begotten of the Father.

* And blessed forever, she brought forth for us God and man.

[Lectio6]
In both natures, therefore, the same is the Son of God, taking ours and not losing His own: renewing man in man, remaining unchangeable in Himself. For the Divinity which He shares with the Father suffered no loss of omnipotence, nor did the form of God suffer harm from the form of a servant: for the supreme and eternal essence, which bent itself for the salvation of the human race, indeed transferred us into its glory, but did not cease to be what it was. Hence, when the Only‑begotten of God confesses Himself less than the Father, to whom He says He is equal, He shows in Himself the truth of both forms: that He proves the inferiority of the human, and declares the equality of the divine.

[Lectio7]
A reading from the holy Gospel according to Luke  
!Luke 2:21  
At that time: After eight days were completed, that the Child should be circumcised, His name was called Jesus. And the rest.  
_  
A homily of Saint Ambrose the Bishop  
!Book 2 on Luke chapter 2, near the middle  
The Child is circumcised. Who is this Child, if not He of whom it is said: A Child is born to us, a Son is given to us? He was made under the Law, that He might gain those who were under the Law. That they might present Him to the Lord. What it is to be presented to the Lord in Jerusalem, I would say, unless I had already said it in my commentary on Isaiah. For he who is circumcised from vices is judged worthy of the Lord’s sight; because the eyes of the Lord are upon the just. You see that the whole series of the old Law was a type of the future: for circumcision signifies the cleansing of sins.

[Lectio8]
But because by a certain inclination to sin, the frailty of human flesh and mind is entangled in inextricable vices, therefore by the eighth day of circumcision the future cleansing of all guilt was prefigured in the age of resurrection. For this is that: Every male opening the womb shall be called holy to the Lord. For by the words of the Law the birth of the Virgin was promised. And truly holy, because immaculate. Finally, that He Himself is the one who is signed by the Law, the repeated words of the Angel declare in the same manner: What shall be born, he says, Holy, shall be called the Son of God.

[Lectio9]
For the Lord Jesus alone among all born of woman is holy through all things, who did not feel the contagion of earthly corruption by the newness of an immaculate birth, and by heavenly majesty drove it away. For if we follow the letter: how is every male holy, when it is not hidden that many were most wicked? Was Ahab holy? Were the false prophets holy, whom at Elijah’s prayer the fire of heaven consumed as avengers of divine injury? But He is holy, whom in the figure of the future mystery the pious prescriptions of the divine Law marked; because He alone, for the generating of the people of God, opened the virginal secret of immaculate fruitfulness of holy Mother Church.
