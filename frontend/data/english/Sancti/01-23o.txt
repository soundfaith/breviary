Secreta=Emerentianae

[Lectio93]
Emerentiana, a Roman virgin, foster‑sister of blessed Agnes, still a catechumen, burning with faith and charity, when she vehemently accused the idol‑worshippers raging against the Christians, was stoned by the excited mob. Praying amid her torments at the tomb of Saint Agnes, she was baptized in her own blood, which she steadfastly shed for Christ, and gave her soul to God.
