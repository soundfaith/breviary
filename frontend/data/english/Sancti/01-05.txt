[Lectio4]
A sermon of Saint Augustine the Bishop  
!Sermon 13 on the Season  
Our Lord Jesus Christ, dearest brothers, who is eternally the Creator of all, today by being born of a mother has become our Savior. He is born for us today in time by His will, so that He may lead us to the eternity of the Father. God became man, so that man might become God: so that man might eat the bread of Angels, the Lord of Angels today became man.

[Lectio5]
Today that prophecy is fulfilled which says: Drop down dew, O heavens, from above, and let the clouds rain down the Just One; let the earth be opened and bring forth the Savior. He who made all things became one who could be found by the one who had perished. For thus man confesses in the Psalms: Before I was humbled, I sinned. Man sinned and became guilty; God was born as man so that the guilty might be freed. Man therefore fell, but God descended. Man fell miserably, God descended mercifully; man fell through pride, God descended with grace.

[Lectio6]
O miracles, O wonders, my brothers! The laws of nature are changed in man; God is born, a virgin conceives without a man, the Word of God weds one who knows not man: she is made both mother and virgin at once; made mother, yet incorrupt; a virgin having a son, knowing not man; always closed, yet not barren. For He alone is born without sin, whom without the embrace of man not the concupiscence of the flesh, but the obedience of the mind brought forth.

[Lectio7]
A reading from the holy Gospel according to Matthew  
!Matt 2:19-23  
At that time: When Herod was dead, behold, the Angel of the Lord appeared in a dream to Joseph in Egypt, saying: Arise, and take the Child and His Mother, and go into the land of Israel. And the rest.  
_  
A homily of Saint Jerome the Priest  
!Book 1 Commentary on Matthew 2  
From this passage we understand that not only Herod, but also the priests and scribes at that time had plotted the death of the Lord. He arose and took the Child and His Mother. He did not say: He took his son and his wife, but the Child and His Mother; as a guardian, not a husband.

[Lectio8]
But hearing that Archelaus reigned in Judea in place of Herod his father, he feared to go there. Many fall into error through ignorance of history, thinking that this is the same Herod by whom the Lord was mocked in His Passion, and who is now said to be dead. Therefore that Herod who later made friendship with Pilate is the son of this Herod, the brother of Archelaus.

[Lectio9]
Because He shall be called a Nazarene. If he had set down a fixed example from Scripture, he would never have said: That which was spoken by the prophets; but simply: That which was spoken by the prophet. Now, calling them prophets in the plural, he shows that he did not take the words from Scripture, but the sense. Nazarene is interpreted “holy”; and all Scripture commemorates that the Lord would be holy.
