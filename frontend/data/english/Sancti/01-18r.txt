[Oratio]
Grant, we beseech You, almighty God, that we who celebrate the birthday of blessed Prisca, Your Virgin and Martyr, may both rejoice in the yearly solemnity and profit by the example of such great faith.
$Through our Lord

[Lectio93]
Prisca, a noble Roman virgin, thirteen years of age, under the emperor Claudius, being accused of the Christian faith, was brought by his order to the temple of Apollo to sacrifice to idols. When she detested the act, she was struck with blows and thrown into prison; and being released from there, since she persevered in constancy of faith, she was scourged, smeared with boiling fat, and again shut in prison. After three days she was brought into the amphitheater and exposed to a lion; who, forgetting his ferocity, humbly laid himself at her feet. Afterwards, afflicted with hunger for three days in a dungeon, she was suspended on the rack, torn with iron claws, and thrown into a fire, from which she miraculously escaped unharmed. Finally, outside the City, her head was cut off, and she completed the palm of virginity with the crown of martyrdom. Her body was buried by Christians on the Ostian Way, at the tenth milestone from the City, on the fifteenth day before the Kalends of February.
