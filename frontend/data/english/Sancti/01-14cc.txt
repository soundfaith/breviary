[Lectio93]
Felix, priest of Nola, when he inveighed vehemently against idols, was variously harassed by unbelievers and cast into prison. From there, being led out at night by an Angel, he was ordered to seek Maximinus, bishop of Nola; who, worn out by age and despairing that he could endure the tortures of persecutors, had hidden himself in a forest. When Felix, led by God, reached the place, he found the holy bishop lying on the ground almost dead; whom he revived and lifted onto his shoulders, and brought to a faithful widow to be cared for. But when he again reproved the worshippers of idols for their impiety, an attack was made upon him; fleeing, he hid himself in a narrow space between two walls. The entrance, suddenly covered with spider webs, left no suspicion to anyone of a recently used hiding place. Escaping from there, Felix hid for three months in the house of the pious woman. But when the Church of God began to have peace, returning to Nola, and converting many to the faith of Christ by examples of life, teachings of doctrine, and miracles, he fell asleep in the Lord, having steadfastly refused the episcopate of that city, and was buried near Nola in a place called “in Pincis.”
